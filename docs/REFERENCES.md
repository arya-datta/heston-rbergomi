# References

## Foundational rough volatility
- Gatheral, J., Jaisson, T., Rosenbaum, M. (2018). *Volatility Is Rough*. Quantitative Finance, 18(6), 933–949. — empirical evidence for rough vol.
- Bayer, C., Friz, P., Gatheral, J. (2016). *Pricing Under Rough Volatility*. Quantitative Finance, 16(6), 887–904. — introduces rBergomi.
- Bennedsen, M., Lunde, A., Pakkanen, M. S. (2017). *Hybrid scheme for Brownian semistationary processes*. Finance and Stochastics, 21(4), 931–965. — the hybrid simulation scheme.

## Comprehensive references
- Bayer, C., Friz, P., Fukasawa, M., Gatheral, J., Jacquier, A., Rosenbaum, M. (eds., 2024). *Rough Volatility*. SIAM. The definitive modern reference.
- Gatheral, J. (2006). *The Volatility Surface: A Practitioner's Guide*. Wiley. Chapters 1–6 cover everything pre-rough-vol.

## Heston model
- Heston, S. L. (1993). *A Closed-Form Solution for Options with Stochastic Volatility, with Applications to Bond and Currency Options*. Review of Financial Studies, 6(2), 327–343.
- Andersen, L. (2008). *Simple and Efficient Simulation of the Heston Model*. Journal of Computational Finance, 11(3), 1–42. — the QE scheme.
- Albrecher, H., Mayer, P., Schoutens, W., Tistaert, J. (2007). *The Little Heston Trap*. Wilmott Magazine, January, 83–92. — the branch-cut fix for the characteristic function.

## SVI parameterization
- Gatheral, J. (2004). *A parsimonious arbitrage-free implied volatility parameterization with application to the valuation of volatility derivatives*. Presentation, Global Derivatives Trading & Risk Management.
- Gatheral, J., Jacquier, A. (2014). *Arbitrage-free SVI volatility surfaces*. Quantitative Finance, 14(1), 59–71.

## Carr-Madan FFT
- Carr, P., Madan, D. (1999). *Option valuation using the fast Fourier transform*. Journal of Computational Finance, 2(4), 61–73.

## Variance swap replication
- Demeterfi, K., Derman, E., Kamal, M., Zou, J. (1999). *More than you ever wanted to know about volatility swaps*. Goldman Sachs Quantitative Strategies Research Note.

## Neural network calibration
- Horvath, B., Muguruza, A., Tomas, M. (2019). *Deep Learning Volatility*. Quantitative Finance.
- Bayer, C., Stemper, B. (2018). *Deep calibration of rough stochastic volatility models*. arXiv:1810.03399.

## Adjacent / further reading
- Euch, O. E., Rosenbaum, M. (2019). *The characteristic function of rough Heston models*. Mathematical Finance, 29(1), 3–38.
- Guyon, J., Lekeufack, J. (2023). *Volatility Is (Mostly) Path-Dependent*. Quantitative Finance.
